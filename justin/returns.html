Returns & Care

Last updated · TEMPLATE — review with counsel before launch

Each JUSTIN piece is finished by hand and sealed before dispatch. Should something not align with your expectations, the Maison offers the following service.

1. Return window

You may return unworn pieces in their original sealed condition within fourteen days of receipt. EU residents are protected by the statutory right of withdrawal in addition to this policy.

2. Eligible items

  • Pieces must be unworn, with all original tags, packaging and serial seal intact.
  • For hygiene reasons, accessories that touch the skin (caps, undergarments) may not be returned once the seal is broken.
  • Drop 001 pieces are numbered and final-sale once registered to your account.

3. How to return

  1. Write to concierge@justin.maison with your order number.
  2. The Maison will issue a return authorisation and prepaid label within 24 hours.
  3. Pack the piece in its original sealed delivery and hand it to the carrier.
  4. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of receipt.

4. Exchanges

Exchanges depend on remaining stock within the drop. Where the requested size is unavailable, we will issue a refund.

5. Care guide

  • Cotton: cold wash, inside out, line dry.
  • Wool / heavy knit: dry clean only.
  • Hardware: polish with a soft dry cloth.

6. Lifetime atelier care

Every JUSTIN piece carries a lifetime repair guarantee. Send the piece to the atelier in Florence and we will restore it at cost, regardless of when it was purchased. Contact atelier@justin.maison for instructions.

7. Contact

The Maison is reachable around the clock at concierge@justin.maison.
