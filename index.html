01 / MANIFESTO

We don't design clothes.
We design silhouettes that move through cities at 3AM —
a language stitched in shadow, cut in light.

EST. 2021

From a single studio in old Delhi to capsule drops shipping to 38 cities. No seasons. Only moods.

METHOD

Hand-finished. Stone-washed. Burned at the edges. Every piece carries a serial — a memory in cotton.

FOR

The night runners. The 4AM thinkers. The ones who treat the city as a wardrobe.
