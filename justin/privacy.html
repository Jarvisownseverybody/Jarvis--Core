JUSTIN Maison ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website or place an order, how we use it, and the rights you hold under applicable law (GDPR for EU residents, CCPA for California residents).

1. Who we are

JUSTIN Maison is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], registered at [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For privacy questions: privacy@justin.maison.

2. Data we collect

  • You give us: email address, shipping address, billing details, order history, optional account credentials.
  • Automatically: device type, browser, IP address, pages viewed, referral source.
  • Cookies: session, preferences, analytics, advertising (see Cookies section).

3. How we use your data

  • Process orders, payments, and deliveries.
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping, returns).
  • Send marketing emails only with your explicit consent.
  • Improve the site and prevent fraud.
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, consumer protection).

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of: contract performance (fulfilling your order), consent (marketing), legitimate interest (fraud prevention, analytics), and legal obligation (tax records).

5. Sharing and processors

We share data only with processors needed to run the Maison: payment (Stripe), email (Klaviyo/Resend), shipping carriers, analytics. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact privacy@justin.maison. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use first-party cookies for cart and session, and third-party cookies for analytics and advertising (with consent). You can manage preferences in your browser or in our cookie banner.

8. Data retention

Order data is retained for the period required by tax law (typically 10 years in the EU). Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is anonymised after 26 months.

9. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

10. Changes

We will post any material changes here and, where required, notify you by email.
